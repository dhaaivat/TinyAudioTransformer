    It has no "unknown word" or "silence" class: anything you say gets mapped to its closest of these 35. Low confidence is the hint that it's guessing.

    About the model

    4-layer transformer over 16×16 spectrogram patches (AST-style, 0.85M parameters), trained from scratch with time shift, SpecAugment and background-noise augmentation. On the official speaker-disjoint test set: 93.4% on clean audio, 88.1% with noise at 5 dB SNR, 83.1% at 0 dB. Most confused: tree↔three, go→no, forward↔four.

    The feature extraction (Kaldi log-mel filterbank) is part of the ONNX graph, so the browser computes exactly what training did. Exported outputs match PyTorch to within 3·10⁻⁵ on 8,000 real clips.
